ABOUT TECHNO INDUSTRIES

Practical expertise.
Value-added packaging.

Techno Industries is an ISO-certified manufacturer specializing in value-added corrugated packaging for local and international markets. Part of Techno Group, the company combines technical expertise, manufacturing technology and food-grade certified materials to produce packaging with durability, structural integrity and product presentation in mind.

A focus on quality

Our approach brings together technical expertise, food-grade certified materials and strict quality control, with continuous improvement at the heart of our work.

WHY CHOOSE TECHNO INDUSTRIES

Built on the details that matter

Materials, structure, print and finishing considered together for your packaging requirements.

A choice of materials

F, E, B, C, BC and EB flutes, with single-, double- and triple-wall board options.

Printing expertise

Flexographic printing in 1–4 colours, offset, line and multi-colour retail printing.

Custom structures

Die-cut, self-locking, partitioned, telescopic and other box formats.

Quality control

Strict quality control and continuous improvement guide our manufacturing approach.

Material considerations

Food-grade, recycled and moisture-resistant options for different applications.

Finishing options

OP varnish, UV and matte UV lamination, with structural enhancements.
