OUR KEY CAPABILITIES

Corrugated Materials.
Print. Structure. Finish.

Bring your product, dimensions and artwork to the team. Review the manufacturing options and define a suitable packaging specification.

PrintingFlexographic, offset, line and multi-colour printing.+Show printing details

Our printing capabilities include flexographic printing in 1–4 colours, offset printing, high-quality line printing and retail-grade multi-colour printing. Food-safe inks are available for produce and FMCG packaging.

Die-CuttingCustom shapes, openings and carton structures.+Show die-cutting details

Die-cut corrugated boxes, custom shapes, perforations, ventilation holes and punch / handle structures support a range of packaging applications.

Custom StructuresCarton formats and internal supports for your product.+Show custom structures details

Explore RSC and HSC cartons, wrap-arounds, telescopic and self-locking boxes, partitioned boxes, punch / handle boxes, pallet / bulk boxes and display boxes / racks.

FinishingOP varnish, UV lamination and structural enhancements.+Show finishing details

Surface options include OP varnish, UV lamination and UV matte lamination. Structural enhancements include die-cutting, perforations, ventilation holes and reinforced edges and corners.
